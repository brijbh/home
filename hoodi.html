Prompt work is messier than it looks.

What starts as one idea becomes versions, edits, comparisons, and results you can't reproduce. Hoodi gives prompts the same structure as any serious working asset — organised, tested, versioned, and understood over time.

01 · Lab

Define. Run. Compare. Decide.

Write a prompt, select your AI providers, and run them all at once. Results appear side by side so you can compare outputs, spot differences, and choose the best path forward.

02 · Library

Every version, scored and stored.

Each prompt you save is versioned and scored automatically. The full history of what you tried, how it changed, and how quality shifted across iterations — nothing disappears.

03 · Builder

Answer five questions. Get a structured prompt.

Builder guides you through a short set of questions and assembles a well-formed, ready-to-run prompt. Useful when you know what you want but aren't sure how to shape it.
