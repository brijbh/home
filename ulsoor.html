Kolam Designs

Ulsoor

A design exploration inspired by kolam, symmetry, and generative form.

Overview

Ulsoor is a project rooted in curiosity about traditional kolam patterns and how they might be explored through digital tools. It sits at the meeting point of geometry, culture, visual rhythm, and interaction design.

Rather than treating kolam as just decorative output, Ulsoor is interested in the logic behind the form: the dots, the flow, the balance, the repetition, and the quiet complexity that gives the pattern life.

What the product is trying to explore

Kolam patterns are beautiful, but they are also systematic. That makes them a powerful design problem. Ulsoor explores what happens when a traditional visual language is approached through tools, structure, experimentation, and interface design.

The goal is not simply to digitize an old form, but to create a respectful, curious space for learning, generating, and understanding pattern behavior.

Core ideas

  • Geometry and symmetry as generative rules.
  • Cultural patterns treated with care and curiosity.
  • A calm visual interface that makes exploration feel inviting.
  • Design as a bridge between tradition and modern tooling.

My role

Ulsoor reflects my long-standing interest in patterns, systems, and the way traditional forms can inspire modern product thinking. I shaped the concept, interaction approach, naming, and visual direction, while AI-assisted development helped bring the implementation forward.
