Pattern Generation

Domlur

A playful generator for visual patterns, rhythm, and repeatable compositions.

Overview

Domlur is a pattern generator built around visual experimentation. It explores how simple rules, repeated shapes, and controlled randomness can create compositions that feel expressive, playful, and alive.

The project is less about one fixed output and more about a space for discovery: a place where design, behavior, and pattern logic can be adjusted, tested, and enjoyed.

What the product is trying to explore

Pattern-making can quickly become either too rigid or too chaotic. Domlur is interested in the middle ground: where structure gives direction, but experimentation still has room to surprise.

It is also part of a broader design interest in systems that are simple to use but capable of producing rich, varied visual outcomes.

Core ideas

  • Simple controls that lead to rich visual results.
  • Repeatability without monotony.
  • Generative design as a playful creative tool.
  • A UI that supports experimentation rather than getting in the way.

My role

Domlur reflects my interest in design systems, creative tooling, and the pleasure of seeing visual ideas come to life through interaction. I shaped the concept, product direction, experience flow, and visual intent, and AI-assisted development helped move the implementation forward.
