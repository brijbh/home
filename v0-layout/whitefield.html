Every photo contains a palette waiting to be named.

Colors in the real world are everywhere — in textures, light, and shadow. But turning a photo into something a designer can use has always required too many steps. Whitefield makes that process immediate, readable, and calm — from image to palette in one place.

01 · Upload

Drop a photo. Get colors.

Upload any image and Whitefield identifies the key colors present — organized by visual weight and presence. No sliders, no manual picking. The image does the work.

02 · Palette

Colors you can actually use.

Each extracted color comes with its hex value, a readable name, and a sense of how it sits in the image. The result is a palette that feels considered, not just computed.

My role

I shaped the product framing, interaction approach, and visual direction — from naming to how the results are displayed. The color extraction logic and development were built with AI assistance.
